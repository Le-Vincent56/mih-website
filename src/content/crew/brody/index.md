Brody is our team's Level and Gameplay Designer. As a long-time lover of all kinds of games, he has a wide range of experience with different genres that fuel his design decisions. At Made In House, Brody aims to create fun and engaging mechanics and gameplay loops that teach themselves. He is primarily responsible for all aspects of the design process, but as we are a small team he isn't the only one coming up with ideas-and he believes we are better because of it. Brody works to constantly improve upon the ideas and systems created by both himself and the other members of Made In House. He believes iteration is the most important part of the design process and that there is only so much you can do without hands-on playtesting. Above all, however, Brody is thankful that he is able to create unique, creative games with his closest friends.
