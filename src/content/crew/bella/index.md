Bella is the artist for Made In House, bringing to life their characters, environments, and promotional assets. She greatly enjoys character design and illustration, favoring fun designs, bold colors and dynamic poses in her work. She has been drawing from the moment she learned to hold a pencil, and since being introduced to the medium of digital art, has shifted her work to video game art, bridging the gap between her love of art and games.
