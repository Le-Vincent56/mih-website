## The Valley

This Spirit World molds to reflect the emotions of the one keeping the spirits happy and safe. The one who maintains balance in the Spirit World: the Caretaker.

Follow Anari on her journey through the Valley, a beautiful sprawling forest, where every secret she uncovers draws her deeper into the world's mysterious magic. As Anari, players will use athletic movement and a magical mushroom mechanic to explore the world, each action of her journey leaving a constructive result on the slowly corrupting forest around her.

Meet spirits, each with their own magical characteristics and unique personalities; some will hinder your journey, others will guide it. But, along the way, Anari might just find out how to unite them all, and restore the balance only a Caretaker can uphold.

## A new balance

Good Luck Valley diversifies the adventure-platformer experience. With modern, genre-defining games such as Hollow Knight, Ori and the Blind Forest, and Gris, there is typically a trade-off within their gameplay.

Exploring Hollow Knight's enchanting and expansive world requires a higher level of skill revolving around its combat system; the engaging and varied gameplay of Ori and the Blind Forest lacks world-incorporated feedback; the cinematic and emotional experience of Gris requires a slower and more thoughtful game loop.

Good Luck Valley amends the sacrifices of these games to provide an evocative and accessible experience. It retains the magical world of Hollow Knight with a lower skill ceiling. It emulates the versatile gameplay of Ori and the Blind Forest while providing immersive, world-affecting feedback. And it derives the cinematics of Gris within an involving and athletic atmosphere.

## The look

Good Luck Valley's aesthetic is inspired by Spirited Away, directed by Hayao Miyazaki; Hollow Knight, developed by Team Cherry; and Neva, developed by Nomada Studios. Together they shape a mystical and inviting world with a rich sense of history and a tinge of mystery.

The Spirit World is crafted using curved lines to create a natural game world filled with personified spirits with unique and exciting interactions. Soft lighting provides a sense of enchanting safety: the forest is a lush, green, and reactive environment that gives the player the feeling of childlike wonder.
